ATS Scoring & Optimization Guide
How Applicant Tracking Systems calculate your match percentage
1
Keyword Exact Match (40% Weight)

ATS scanners tokenize job descriptions to extract hard skills (e.g., Python, Docker, PostgreSQL) and required tooling. Use the exact technical terminology in your skills and project bullets.

2
Quantified Achievements (25% Weight)

Bullets containing concrete metrics (e.g., "Reduced API latency by 42%" or "Processed 1M+ daily events") score significantly higher than vague responsibility statements.

3
Format & Clean Parsing (20% Weight)

Use single-column layouts with standard section headings (Experience, Skills, Education, Projects). Avoid tables, text boxes, and multi-column graphics that break OCR parsers.

4
Action Verbs & Impact (15% Weight)

Begin bullet points with strong power action verbs (Architected, Engineered, Optimized, Deployed, Speared) to demonstrate leadership and execution capability.

Real-Time AI Technical Interview Tips
Proven strategies to ace technical and behavioral rounds
1. The STAR Method for Behavioral & System Questions

Structure every response into Situation (context), Task (objective), Action (your exact technical contributions), and Result (measurable outcome and business impact).

2. Speech Pacing & Eliminating Filler Words

Maintain an ideal speaking rate of 120 - 150 Words Per Minute. When you need a moment to think, pause silently for 2 seconds instead of saying "um", "uh", or "like".

3. Think Out Loud in Coding & Architecture

Explain your thought process before writing code: state the brute-force baseline, identify bottlenecks, propose an optimal O(N) approach using Hash Maps/Two Pointers, and clarify edge cases.

4. Body Language & Video Presence

Position your camera at eye level, maintain eye contact with the lens rather than looking down, ensure good lighting on your face, and use hand gestures naturally when explaining architecture diagrams.
